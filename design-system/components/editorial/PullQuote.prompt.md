Editorial pull quote — gold left-rule, faint gold tint, Playfair italic body, mono uppercase source line. Use to lift a notable quotation out of body copy.

```jsx
<PullQuote source="Heber C. Kimball, letter to Parley P. Pratt, June 1842">
  Masonry was taken from preasthood but has become degenerated, but menny things are perfect.
</PullQuote>
```

Pass the quote as children and the attribution via `source`.
