Content card with hairline border, soft shadow, and the signature left accent rule. The base container for intro boxes and feature panels.

```jsx
<Card rule="crimson">
  <p>The LDS temple is presented as the most sacred space on earth…</p>
</Card>
<Card tone="dark" rule="none">A House Built on Sand</Card>
```

`rule`: `crimson` (default), `gold`, `none`. `tone`: `light` (default white), `alt` (warm #F8F4F0), `dark` (maroon gradient, text turns parchment). `padded` toggles the 28×32 inset.
